## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for high-impact Below-The-Line (BTL) activations, experiential marketing agencies, and corporate event management solutions. The aesthetic merges executive corporate authority with dynamic, on-the-ground operational precision.

### Aesthetic Foundation: Flat Design 2.0 (Semi-Flat)
The design language rejects skeuomorphic excesses and pure flat minimalism in favor of Flat Design 2.0:
- **Functional Hierarchy:** Crisp surface layers differentiated through deliberate borders and subtle ambient micro-elevations rather than heavy drop shadows.
- **Graphic Directness:** Bold, structural geometry balanced by approachable radii.
- **High-Impact Contrast:** Authoritative deep indigos combined with high-visibility signal accents (crimson and cyan) to drive clear conversion funnels for lead capture and direct messaging.
- **Operational Rigor:** Clean data tables, structured event timelines, quantifiable metric cards, and tactile CTAs tailored for enterprise decision-makers.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate corporate gravitas balanced by purposeful, high-energy accents calibrated for lead-generation and operational status tracking.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary Indigo (`#312782`):** Corporate cornerstone. Applied to primary display headlines (H1/H2), dominant navigational frames, structural borders, and authority accents.
- **Accent Crimson (`#AB2020`):** Reserved exclusively for conversion-driven primary calls-to-action, high-priority highlights, and urgent promotional prompts.
- **Secondary Cyan (`#009EE2`):** Used for micro-accents, interactive icons, hover indicators, secondary tags, and metric indicators.
- **Graphite Dark (`#1A1413`):** High-legibility neutral tone applied across body copy, heavy typography, and grounding structural containers like footers.

### Specialized & Surface Tones
- **Operational Lime (`#93C01F`):** Functional accent for operational status badges, active indicators, and success states.
- **Direct Connect Green (`#25D366`):** Reserved strictly for WhatsApp interactive targets and immediate direct sales contact triggers.
- **Surface Light (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast base for primary cards, modals, and spotlight modules.
- **Surface Muted (`#F8F9FA`):** Low-contrast structural tint for alternating canvas sections, input backdrops, and table strips.
- **Subtle Stroke (`#E5E7EB` / `rgb(229, 231, 235)`): Neutral border definition creating the foundation of Flat Design 2.0 component edges.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements **Sora** for structural framing and impact, and **Manrope** for sustained editorial clarity.

- **Headings & Titles (Sora):** Technical, progressive geometric sans-serif. Applied in weights `600` (SemiBold) and `700` (Bold) with tight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) on display sizes to convey confidence and enterprise structure.
- **Body & Subtitles (Manrope):** Modern, balanced humanist grotesque. Provides open counters and clean rhythm for high readability in body paragraphs, technical briefs, and data displays. Applied in `400` (Regular) and `500` (Medium).
- **Badges & Interactive Triggers:** Set exclusively in Sora (`600` or `700`) to guarantee high-density legibility within buttons, inputs, and uppercase status badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an enterprise 12-column grid anchored to a maximum width of `1280px` for landing pages, alternating between pristine white canvas segments and muted technical bands.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Grids
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column fluid grid, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and a minimum outer margin of `2rem` (32px).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `1.5rem` (24px) margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1rem` (16px) canvas edge padding.

### Rhythm & Alignment
Component internals follow a disciplined 4px/8px modular scale. Spacing tokens strictly delineate layout proximity: `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) govern tight pairings like icon-to-label offsets, `space-md` (16px) defines input pads and card insets, while `space-lg` (24px) and `space-xl` (40px) manage card internal separation and module division.

## Elevation & Depth

In alignment with Flat Design 2.0 principles, visual depth is structural, tactile, and restrained. Heavy multi-layered drop shadows and skeuomorphic gradients are replaced by clean boundaries and purposeful micro-elevations.

### Surface Architecture
- **Boundary Precision:** Surfaces predominantly rely on thin, high-clarity structural borders (`1px solid #F3F4F6` or `#E5E7EB`) to define perimeter boundaries rather than ambient depth alone.
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background layer utilizing alternating `#FFFFFF` and `#F8F9FA` fields. Zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** Baseline resting cards use pure white fills paired with a subtle, diffuse ambient shadow: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Focus Elevation):** Interactive elements rise gently on hover to `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)` over a 300ms transition curve, signaling interactivity without visual noise.
- **Modals & Overlays:** Floating navigational bars and popovers leverage `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)` surrounded by a `#E5E7EB` outline.

## Shapes

The geometric signature uses controlled, professional corner radii:

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** `rounded-2xl` (1rem / 16px) delivers a modern, approachable container silhouette without sacrificing geometric authority.
- **Buttons, Inputs & Interactive Selectors:** `rounded-xl` (0.75rem / 12px) creates an ergonomic, clickable profile.
- **Badges, Tags & Avatars:** Fully rounded `rounded-full` (9999px) capsules contrast sharply against square column grids and structural cards.

## Components

### Primary Buttons (CTA)
- **Style:** Background `#AB2020`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `Sora` SemiBold (`label-md`).
- **Sizing & Radii:** Rounded-xl (`12px`), horizontal padding `1.5rem` (24px), vertical padding `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Interactions:** Hover state transitions to `#8F1B1B` over 200ms; focus ring displays an offset outline in `#AB2020` with 2px gap.

### WhatsApp Action Buttons
- **Style:** Background `#25D366`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `Sora` SemiBold (`label-md`). 
- **Layout:** Flex container aligning the official icon and label with an `8px` gap.
- **Sizing & Radii:** Rounded-xl (`12px`), horizontal padding `1.5rem`, vertical padding `0.75rem`.
- **Interactions:** Hover state darkens to `#20BA5A` over 200ms with a micro-scale of 1.01.

### Badges & Pill Indicators
- **Structure:** Rounded-full (`9999px`), padding horizontal `0.75rem` (12px), vertical `0.25rem` (4px).
- **Typography:** `label-sm` (Sora, 11px, Bold, uppercase, tracking `0.08em`).
- **Operational Badge:** Background `rgba(147, 192, 31, 0.12)`, text `#5C7C0B`, border `1px solid rgba(147, 192, 31, 0.3)`.
- **Secondary / Micro Badge:** Background `rgba(0, 158, 226, 0.1)`, text `#007AB0`, border `1px solid rgba(0, 158, 226, 0.25)`.

### Cards & Container Panels
- **Structure:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface, `16px` border radius (`rounded-2xl`), border `1px solid #F3F4F6`.
- **Elevation:** Resting `shadow-sm`, escalating to `shadow-md` and border `#E5E7EB` upon cursor hover over a 300ms transition.
- **Internal Spacing:** `24px` (`space-lg`) on mobile, expanding to `32px` on desktop viewport sizes.

### Input Fields
- **Structure:** Background `#FFFFFF` (or `#F8F9FA` on white cards), border `1px solid #E5E7EB`, radius `rounded-xl` (`12px`).
- **Typography:** Body font `Manrope` (`body-md`), text color `#1A1413`, placeholder color `#9CA3AF`.
- **States:** Focus rings replace the standard outline with a crisp 2px border in Brand Indigo (`#312782`) with zero drop shadow.

### Metric / KPI Blocks
- **Structure:** Crisp white tiles with an accent left-border (3px) in `#009EE2` or `#312782`.
- **Content:** Sora Bold for large analytical numerals paired with Manrope Medium uppercase descriptors.
